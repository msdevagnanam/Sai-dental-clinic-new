About the Clinic

Meet Dr. Rithani,
Chief Dental Surgeon

Dr. Rithani founded Sai Dental Clinic in Kodumudi in 2022, and has been its Chief Dental Surgeon ever since. She is a registered dentist with the Dental Council of India, a member of the Indian Dental Association, and part of Colgate's Oral Health Movement. She completed her BDS at Rajas Dental College and Hospital (2010–2015, under The Tamil Nadu Dr. MGR Medical University), followed by fellowships in clinical endodontics at Mohan's Micro Dentistry and oral surgery at Prabhu Dental Hospital. Before founding the clinic, she practised at the I-tooth chain of dental hospitals in Karur (2015–2017) and Tamara Dental Clinic in Chennai (2018–2019) — bringing close to a decade of experience to every patient in Kodumudi.

School Dental Screening Program

Healthy Smiles, Better Learning.

A comprehensive dental screening program for school children.

Dr. Rithani K.R BDS., FCE. | Dental Surgeon | Kodumudi

Why dental screening is important for school going children?

Foundation for Growth

School is not only for academic learning but also a foundation for developing discipline, communication, and teamwork. Schools prepare children to become confident members of society.

Impact of Poor Health

Tooth pain makes it difficult to concentrate. Bad breath and decayed teeth reduce self-confidence, making children hesitant to participate in discussions and leading to absenteeism.

Investment in the Future

Healthy students learn and participate better. Early dental screening identifies problems fast, acting as a crucial investment in a child's health, academic success, and overall well-being.

Highlights:

  • Non-invasive dental caries screening
  • Assessment of oral hygiene and gum health
  • Early screening for malocclusion and other harmful oral habits
  • Recommendations for preventive and corrective treatment
  • Each student will receive individual dental report
  • Oral health education session

Oral health education session:

  • Brushing techniques
  • Importance of flossing
  • Importance of regular dental visits
  • Importance of healthy food habits

Benefits for schools and students:

  • Health promotion initiative shows schools care and concern for students, which creates a positive impact on school among parents
  • Professional dental health program improves performance among students
  • Improves oral hygiene awareness among students, teachers and parents
  • Awareness video for parents
